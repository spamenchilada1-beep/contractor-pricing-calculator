Contractor Pricing Markup Guide

Use this guide to understand the relationship between job cost, markup, selling price, gross profit, and gross margin before you price a construction job.

How contractor markup works

Markup is the percentage added to your cost to arrive at a selling price.

Selling price = job cost × (1 + markup ÷ 100)

Example: $10,000 job cost with 20% markup

A $10,000 job cost with a 20% markup produces a $12,000 selling price. Gross profit is $2,000. Gross margin is 16.67% because gross margin is measured against the selling price, not the original cost.

Markup vs. gross margin

Markup and gross margin are related but different measurements. Markup is based on cost. Gross margin is gross profit divided by the final selling price.

Use your actual job cost

The calculator only works from the cost you enter. Build a pricing basis that reflects the costs you are trying to recover, then use the resulting selling price as a pricing check.
